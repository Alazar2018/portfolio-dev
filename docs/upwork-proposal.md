## Proposal tips (Upwork-specific)

| Do | Avoid |
|----|--------|
| Mention something **specific** from their job post in line 1 | Generic "I am expert developer" openings |
| Propose **2–4 clear milestones** with outcomes | Vague "I will do my best" |
| Link **one** relevant live project | Dumping 10 unrelated links |
| Ask **one** smart question at the end | Interrogating them with 5 questions |
| Match their budget tone (fixed vs hourly) | Bidding without reading budget type |

**Boosters (when allowed):**  
- Attach a 1-page PDF or link to a relevant case study from your portfolio.  
- If the job is fixed-price, quote milestone 1 only — easier to win than quoting the entire vague scope.  
- Reply within the first few hours of posting when the job is fresh.
