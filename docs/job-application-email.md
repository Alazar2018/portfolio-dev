## Customization checklist

Before sending, replace every `[bracket]` field and:

1. **Use the hiring manager’s name** when you can find it on LinkedIn or the job post.
2. **Mirror 2–3 keywords** from the job description (stack, seniority, domain).
3. **Name one real project** that matches the role (fintech/payments → Arifpay; inventory/SaaS → Inventra Cloud; CMS → Noah Real Estate / Sinopia; full-stack → Ticketr or parking system).
4. **Keep the email under ~200 words** unless the role is senior/lead and warrants more detail.
5. **Attach a PDF resume** named `Alazar-Tilahun-Resume.pdf` (or the file from your portfolio: `/cv/Alazar TIlahun Professional Resume.pdf`).
6. **Send Tuesday–Thursday, morning** in the employer’s timezone when possible.
